What each column means

Tag columns are short on purpose; detail is in Notes.

  • Functional endpoint — pre-specified measure of how the patient functions. Survival and Not a functional scale are separate tags (labs, bleeding, imaging).
  • Treated n — size of the treated group in the main analysis. The filter keeps that size or smaller. Control counts are in Notes. Rows marked Not found stay hidden while a ceiling is set.
  • Biomarker — Yes if a marker tracked biology in that program; Endpoint if the marker is the endpoint. Company figures are labeled in Notes.
  • AdCom — No may mean FDA said no meeting, or (for 2016–2026 products) absence from this project’s meeting list. Pending — none held, and one may still be planned. Not found — approval before 4 Nov 2016; letter not checked.
  • Not found — the FDA documents read for this row do not state the field.
How rows were chosen (inclusion)

A product is listed only if it has an FDA orphan designation for the indication and the efficacy package used the natural-history class in the project README. That class is subject-level data from a natural history study, medical records, or a registry, for the untreated course of the disease. A literature rate, a chart review, another trial’s arm, and the patient’s own baseline are not that class.

Approval is not required. Sample size, significance, and AdCom are columns, not entry rules. Original approvals from 1 Nov 2022 through 19 Nov 2026 were screened. The log is screen_log.csv. This is not a full screen of every orphan drug or every efficacy supplement.

Method and sources

Built from two published reviews, then from CDER novel approvals, CBER biological approvals, and openFDA complete-response letters through 19 Nov 2026. The class is in the project README. Each row was filled from an FDA letter, review, label, or snapshot unless marked as company data.

  1. Jahanshahi et al. — external controls in FDA approvals 2000–2019; kept rare-disease rows with retrospective natural history and orphan designation.
  2. Vaghela et al. — non-oncologic orphan NDAs/BLAs Jan 2017–Oct 2022; kept the natural-history bucket, not chart reviews and not another study’s arm.
  3. CBER RWE list — cross-check, not the list. Examples already in the table: Zolgensma, Rethymic, Kebilidi.
  4. Forward screen, 1 Nov 2022 through 19 Nov 2026: CDER novel-drug pages, CBER BLA pages, and the openFDA complete-response set (100 letters, last updated 18 Nov 2026). New rows from that screen: Lembaz, Zycubo, Kygevvi, Skyclarys, Miplyffa, Yuviwel, and the Brineura under-3 supplement.
  5. AdCom No for products absent from this project’s meeting files (4 Nov 2016–present). ~19 meetings vs Daval et al. remain unidentified.

Excluded by design
  • Patient as own baseline (Hemgenix, Roctavian, Beqvez).
  • Placebo from another trial (Vutrisiran).
  • Historical treated series, not untreated course (Tepadina, pretomanid regimens).
  • Concurrent control, not chart-as-NH (Luxturna: fellow eye).
  • Not an orphan population (Lantidra).
  • Concurrent placebo pivotal (Anascorp).
Names discussed but not listed
  • Iwilfin — ANBL0032 is a prior trial arm, not untreated natural history (FDA approval note).
  • Chart reviews (Dojolvi, Diacomit, Mepsevii, Luxturna) — Vaghela et al. class; FDA did not put them in labels as NH efficacy.
  • Lumizyme, Nexviazyme, Pombiliti — pivotal comparisons were randomized; infantile Myozyme row covers NH Pompe.
  • Oncology single-arm ORR — out of scope (Vaghela excluded oncology).
  • Zavesca — US Gaucher indication, not NPC.
  • Lamzede — Beck et al. 2013 is cited to describe alpha-mannosidosis. The pivotal trial was randomized (FDA integrated review, BLA 761278).
  • Aqneursa — the integrated review for NDA 219132 uses natural-history papers to interpret the scale. It does not compare patients with a subject-level untreated cohort.
  • Xenpozyme — the adult trial was randomized. Pediatric efficacy was extrapolated from that trial. ASCEND-Peds was not an untreated-cohort comparison (FDA dose-escalation summary).
  • Yartemlea — the control FDA used is a literature response rate. Jahanshahi counts that as published data, not retrospective natural history. The Kyoto registry comparison was called inconclusive (FDA integrated review, BLA 761152).
  • Ctexli — confirmatory natural-history material is published literature on the disease course, not a subject-level untreated cohort (FDA LEADER 3D).
  • Evrysdi — the summary review treats a literature performance threshold as the external control for FIREFISH, not a matched subject-level cohort (NDA 213535 summary review).
  • Elevidys — external-control data, including CINRG, were in the file. A secondary account of the Center Director’s memo says they were not helpful, and approval rested on the randomized study. The summary basis was not re-read for a tally, so there is no row.
  • Kresladi — FDA did not accept the historical survival benchmark. Approval used neutrophil CD18/CD11a expression (summary basis, 26 Mar 2026).
  • Dichloroacetate (Saol, NDA 218679) — the 27 Aug 2025 complete response letter says a published 2012 natural-history paper could not be used as the survival comparison.
  • Waskyra — severe infections were compared with the patient’s own pre-treatment rate (FDA label).
  • Deramiocel (BLA 125842) — the pivotal evidence was the randomized HOPE-3 trial. The file compared the HOPE-2 open-label extension with external upper-limb and cardiac MRI controls; FDA did not find these interpretable, and the cardiac controls were on cardiac medication (FDA briefing, 29 Jul 2026). Complete response letters 9 Jul 2025 and 20 Nov 2026 (openFDA).
